Plate boundaries: PB2002
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Peter Bird (2003), An updated digital model of plate boundaries, Geochemistry,
Geophysics, Geosystems 4(3):1027 (PB2002). GIS conversion by Hugo Ahlenius, Nordpil
(https://github.com/fraxen/tectonicplates).

Licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/
You are free to copy, distribute and use the data, to produce works from it and to
modify, transform and build upon it, as long as you attribute it as above. EarthWatch3D
merges the steps into polylines by kind and simplifies them to 0.01 degrees.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Globe imagery: NASA Blue Marble
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation with Topography and Bathymetry
(July 2004, R. Stockli), resized to 8192x4096. NASA imagery is generally not
copyrighted (NASA Media Usage Guidelines).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Coastlines and borders: Natural Earth
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), 1:50m coastline and land boundary
lines. Public domain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Live data
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Earthquakes: U.S. Geological Survey. Tropical cyclones: NOAA National Hurricane Center
and GDACS (European Commission and United Nations). Weather alerts: NOAA National
Weather Service. Severe storms: NASA EONET. Lightning: NOAA GOES Geostationary
Lightning Mapper. Clouds: NASA GIBS (VIIRS NOAA-21 true colour).
